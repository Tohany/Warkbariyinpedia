Sultan Tohan Ishaq Aladinicz I.

Tohan Ishaq Aladinicz is the first sultan and has been the sultan of Warkbar since January 2026. He is the president and founder of the Warkbarian democratic party (WDP). Tohan is also the supreme leader of Warkbar. He is known for standardizing the Warkbarian language and writing the first Warkbarian-English dictionary.
